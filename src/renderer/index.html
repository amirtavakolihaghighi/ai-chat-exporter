Where your files are kept

…

Sign-ins to chat sites and the built-in browser's cache are not kept here. They stay in Windows' app-data folder, where the browser locks them while the app runs — away from files you might share or back up somewhere public.

Everything runs locally. Nothing is uploaded, and the app makes no network requests of its own — only the pages you load do.
